How it works

Three small steps, at your own pace
01

Snap

Take a clear photo of what's bothering you. Dermi guides you on light and distance so the picture is actually useful.

02

Talk to Dermi

Dermi asks gentle follow-up questions — when it started, what changed, how it feels — and builds a case summary as you chat.

03

Track healing

Each concern becomes a case with photos, pain scores and reminders, so you can see it getting better over time.

Safety first

Dermi supports, not replaces, a dermatologist.

Dermi helps you understand what you're seeing, keep track of changes and prepare for an appointment. It can't diagnose you or prescribe treatment.

Red flags
If something looks serious — spreading fast, a fever, a mole that's changing — Dermi says so calmly and clearly.
Doctor handoff
Photos, timeline and symptoms in one printable report your dermatologist can read in a minute.
Your call
Dermi never closes a consultation on its own. You decide when you're cared for.

## Rules

- **User-uploaded images and conversations** are personal health data. They are never used for training unless a separate
  consent flow and an ADR exist. They are stored only in Supabase (private bucket, RLS), never in `data/`, DVC or W&B.
- Before adding image datasets, check the license on each one. Many dermatology sets forbid commercial use.
- Synthetic data must be traceable to its generator config (`experiment.name`) and model.
